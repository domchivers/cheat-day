New in this build: renaming an exercise mid-workout works smoothly; after a workout you're asked whether to post it to the feed (with a gym pic if you like), friends can see your workouts in your day if you turn it on in Settings, and the end-of-workout questions show every choice, including Just this once.

Thanks for testing! Please just use it like a normal day:

• Log your meals: try scanning a barcode, taking a photo of a label, and typing things like "2 slices of toast with butter".
• Tap the blue Plan button at the top of Today for meal ideas. Tell it what's in your fridge and how much you fancy cooking.
• Try the Assistant: ask it anything about food or your day.
• If you train, log a workout and use the rest timer.
• Add a weigh-in, or connect Apple Health if you have a smart scale.

If anything looks wrong, confusing or too small to read, take a screenshot. TestFlight then asks if you want to send it as feedback, and you can add a note. Crashes are sent automatically.
