New in this build: on a photo estimate, Smaller and Bigger resize the whole plate, and tapping a part gives it its own − and + and shows the size it was judged at, what it was counted as (tap Change to pick another) and its grams to type over; photo estimates size food against the plate, mug or box in the shot, foods are matched to the right entry (natural cheese, cream cakes, chicken with skin), a named shop's own numbers are looked up first, the same food from the same place starts from what you confirmed last time, and Me and Body show the body fat from your latest weigh-in, the date to your goal says "any day now" when you're nearly there, and the weight chart's label shows the full number.

Thanks for testing! Please just use it like a normal day:

• Log your meals: try scanning a barcode, taking a photo of a label, and typing things like "2 slices of toast with butter".
• Tap the blue Plan button at the top of Today for meal ideas. Tell it what's in your fridge and how much you fancy cooking.
• Try the Assistant: ask it anything about food or your day.
• If you train, log a workout and use the rest timer.
• Add a weigh-in, or connect Apple Health if you have a smart scale.

If anything looks wrong, confusing or too small to read, take a screenshot. TestFlight then asks if you want to send it as feedback, and you can add a note. Crashes are sent automatically.
